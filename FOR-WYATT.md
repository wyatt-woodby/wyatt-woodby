# Editing your website

Everything on your site — videos, photos, and their order — is managed from one
simple page. You don't need to touch any code, and you can't break the site.

**Your editing page:** **wyattwoodby.com/keystatic**

The first time you go there, click **"Log in with GitHub"** and sign in. That's it.

> After you save anything, the website takes **1–2 minutes** to update itself.
> Refresh the page after a couple minutes and your change will be there.

---

## Add a video

1. On the left, click **Media** → then the **Create** button (top right).
2. **Title** — type a name for it. (A "slug" fills in by itself. Ignore it.)
3. **Type** — choose **Vimeo video**.
4. **Vimeo link** — paste the Vimeo link (or the whole embed code — either works).
5. Click **Save**.
6. **Important:** now go to **Feed order** (see below) to make it show on the site.

## Add a photo

1. Click **Media** → **Create**.
2. **Title** — name it.
3. **Type** — choose **Image**.
4. **Upload** the photo file.
5. Click **Save**.
6. **Important:** go to **Feed order** to make it show on the site.

## Change the order (and show new items)

1. On the left, click **Feed order**.
2. **Drag** items up or down into the order you want.
3. To **add** a newly-created video or photo to the site, add it to this list.
4. To **remove** something from the site, remove it from this list.
   (Removing it here just hides it — it's not deleted.)
5. Click **Save**.

> **A video or photo only appears on the site if it's in the Feed order list.**
> Creating it in Media is step one; adding it to Feed order is what publishes it.

---

## Good to know

- **Videos play automatically** (muted, on a loop). Visitors can turn on sound
  using the video's own controls.
- **You can't break it.** Every change is saved safely, and old versions are kept
  automatically. If something looks off, just fix it and save again.
- **Photos live in the site; videos live on Vimeo.** So keep your Vimeo videos in
  your Vimeo account — the site just links to them.
- **Nothing shows up instantly.** Give it 1–2 minutes after saving, then refresh.

That's everything. Log in, create, drag to order, save.
